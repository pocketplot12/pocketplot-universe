What it does

Stories that branch.
Worlds that remember.

Every world is a graph, not a list. Each scene has 2-3 choices that branch the story. The same world can be played hundreds of times with different paths — and every choice you make shapes the next chapter.

Branching

Branching-first narrative

Your world unfolds across connected scenes. Players make choices that lead to new scenes, new paths, and new endings. No two playthroughs are the same.

BYOB

Bring your own brain

Connect your own OpenAI-compatible LLM. The platform handles content moderation on the response side — your model, your guardrails.

Visual

Consistent scene art

Every scene gets a procedurally-composed SVG illustration matched to the genre, tone, and story state. Visual continuity without paying per image.

Modes

Two ways to play every story.

Play

Visual novel. Choices. Branching.

Numbered choice buttons, progress bar, completion banner. Player sessions are tracked so you can return to where you left off.

Read

Manga / storybook. Read-only.

One page per episode. Art panel + italic narration + speech bubbles. Page-flip animation. Keyboard navigation.
