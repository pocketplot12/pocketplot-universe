Privacy & ownership

You do. Always. Worlds are tied to your account. You can delete them, export them, share them, or take them down. PocketPlot never trains on your worlds without your explicit consent.
No. Your worlds, prompts, and edits are private by default. You can opt in to share worlds publicly, which makes them discoverable to other PocketPlot users — but they are never used as model training data.
Your account goes read-only. Your worlds stay accessible for 90 days in case you re-subscribe. After 90 days they're permanently deleted. You can export your worlds at any time before then.
Only Stripe (billing) and your chosen model provider (BYOB). No ad networks. No data brokers. No marketing trackers beyond basic aggregate analytics.
