Your stories are yours. We don't sell them, license them, or use them to train models. Export them whenever you want.

Pro and Creator are billed monthly or annually. Cancel from your dashboard; you'll keep access until the end of the period.

Service outages, security incidents, policy changes — we email you. We'd rather lose a few users to transparency than keep a few by hiding problems.

If you change your mind inside 14 days of a Pro or Creator charge, reply to the receipt email and we'll refund. No questions, no friction.

9. We may change these terms.

When we do, we'll email you at least 14 days in advance. Continued use after the effective date counts as acceptance. If you don't accept, you can cancel and get a prorated refund.
