How it works

From idea to a world in four doors.

PocketPlot Universe is the only writing platform that builds the story with you — branching choices, original art, and an engine you can shape yourself. Here's the full tour.

01

Branch

Every world is a graph, not a list. Each scene has 2–3 choices that fork the story — and every choice shapes what comes next.

02

World

Choose a genre (fantasy, sci-fi, noir, romance, horror, and ten more), a tone, and a setting. The world is yours to name.

03

Seed

Open /seed, pick a hook, and let the engine draft an opening scene. Tweak the prose, change a word, fork a path — your world starts to breathe.

04

Remix

Open any world in your library and fork it. Keep the bones, swap the genre, change the tone. Every world is remixable, nothing is locked.

Two engines. Same story.

Procedural (default, all tiers)

The in-house PocketPlot engine writes a fresh scene from your prompt. The same prompt twice gives two different stories — the engine picks characters, sets the scene, and commits to a direction.

BYOB (Creator tier)

Bring Your Own Brain. Connect any OpenAI-compatible model — your API key, your guardrails, your budget. PocketPlot handles the response-side moderation so the story stays on rails.

The 100 calls/day cap (Creator)

Creator tier gets 100 BYOB calls per day. Enough for a writer who sketches daily, not a spammer. Reset at midnight UTC.
